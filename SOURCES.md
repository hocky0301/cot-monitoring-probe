## Primary papers checked for the brief and omitted from the main argument

**P1. Eric Onyame, Runtao Zhou, Kowshik Thopalli, Bhavya Kailkhura, Chirag Agarwal. _The Fragility of Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Across Typologically Diverse Languages_. May 27, 2026, v1.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27901), [fixed PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27901v1). L2, preprint. Opened abstract, full PDF and rendered Table 6, p.22. Japanese is explicitly included. This answers the brief's language question; it does not establish Astra's Japanese behavior.

**P2. Agatha Duzan, Asa Cooper Stickland. _Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Can Be Unreliable in Implicit-Influence Settings_. August 5, 2026, v1.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04735), [fixed PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04735v1). L2, preprint. Opened abstract, setup and Table 3, p.25. The reported 41–46-point gap is scoped to two of four settings. Its textual detector measures a different endpoint from the user's token count. Numerical corrections are in ERRORS_AND_CORRECTIONS.md.

**P3. Richard J. Young. _Measuring Faithfulness Depends on How You Measure: Classifier Sensitivity in LLM Chain-of-Thought Evaluation_. March 20, 2026; checked v2, March 23.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20172), [fixed PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.20172v2). L2, single-author preprint. Opened abstract, Table 1 p.5, §4.4 p.8/Figure 3 p.9 and limitations p.12. Rates are 74.4%, 82.6%, 69.7%; Qwen3.5-27B changes from 1st to 7th under two classifiers. These are classification results, not established classifier accuracy; no human ground-truth set resolves which definition is correct.

**P4. Melody Y. Guan, Miles Wang, Micah Carroll, Zehao Dou, Annie Y. It does not establish the brief's alleged retraction by a shared author group.

**P5. Chen Yueh-Han, Robert McCarthy, Bruce W. Lee, He He, Ian Kivlichan, Bowen Baker, Micah Carroll, Tomek Korbak. _Reasoning Models Struggle to Control their Chains of Thought_. March 5, 2026, v1.** [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05706), [developer-hosted PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a21c39c1-fa07-41db-9078-973a12620117/cot_controllability.pdf). L2, preprint. Used to check the metric, not to supply Astra-era rates; those come from A1.
